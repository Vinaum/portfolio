Place official company logos here, named by company id:

- `pier.svg` (or .png / .webp)
- `gobots.svg`
- `pagegroup.svg`

They are detected at build time. Without a file, the site shows the company name as a typographic wordmark.
By default logos are rendered in monochrome off-white to sit inside the dark editorial palette;
set `logoTreatment` in src/components/sections/Companies.tsx to "original" to keep brand colours.
